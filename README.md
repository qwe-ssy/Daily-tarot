## 📖 Introduction

**Daily Tarot** is more than just a divination tool—it's a modern tarot learning platform that bridges structured knowledge (Wiki) with persuasive communication and psychological intervention. 

We move away from traditional fatalism, transforming ancient tarot archetypes into daily emotional anchors. Through the intentional ritual of a "single daily draw," paired with minimalist interpretations and actionable micro-steps, it provides lightweight psychological support for users navigating uncertainty. Concurrently, the built-in, comprehensive Tarot Wiki helps learners build a systematic understanding of the cards.

## Core Features

*   **🃏 The Daily Draw (Ritual & Intent):** Designed for high-frequency engagement with a strong sense of ritual. Resets at midnight and is strictly limited to one draw per day. A countdown lock mechanism amplifies the psychological weight and intentionality of each draw.
*   **🧠 Actionable Nudges (Persuasive AI):** Rooted in persuasive communication principles, our AI engine avoids ambiguous prophecies. Instead, it translates card meanings into a clear "daily emotional tone" accompanied by a specific, highly executable "micro-action."
*   **📚 Comprehensive Tarot Wiki (SEO-Optimized):** Powered by a built-in structured JSON database of all 78 cards. Leverages Next.js SSG to generate lightning-fast pages covering upright/reversed meanings, visual symbolism, and multi-context interpretations (Love/Career). 
*   **📔 Private Tarot Journal:** A lightweight local tracking system utilizing Local Storage. It monitors streak data and seamlessly transforms the user's draw history into a private, therapeutic emotional diary.
*   **📸 Social Share & Growth:** One-click generation of highly aesthetic, 9:16 ratio cards optimized for Instagram Stories and social platforms, driving low-cost, organic social loops.
*   **🌍 Bilingual Support:** Seamlessly toggle between English and Chinese. Designed to cater to both global audiences and the vibrant metaphysical community on platforms like Xiaohongshu. (无缝切换中英文双语界面与解析。既能拓展广阔的海外受众，又能精准承接小红书等国内平台的玄学流量。)
